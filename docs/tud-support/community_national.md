## LCRDM
The National Coordination Point Research Data Management (LCRDM) is a national network of experts in the field of research data management (RDM). The LCRDM forms the link between policy and solution. Within the LCRDM, experts work together to put RDM topics on the agenda that require a joint national approach. 

## DANS
[DANS](https://dans.knaw.nl/en/) (Data Archiving and Networked Services) is a Dutch national center of expertise and repository for research data. It supports researchers in managing, archiving, and sharing their data.

## SURF
[SURF](https://www.surf.nl/en) is the collaborative ICT organization for Dutch education and research. It provides services and support for research data management, including infrastructure, training, and consultancy.

## NL-RSE
[NL-RSE](https://nl-rse.org/) is the Dutch community for Research Software Engineers. It aims to support and promote the role of software engineering in research, providing resources, training, and networking opportunities for professionals in this field.

## Open Science NL
[Open Science NL](https://www.opensciencenl.org/) is a community initiative that promotes open science practices in the Netherlands. It brings together researchers, policymakers, and other stakeholders to foster collaboration and share best practices in open science.

## DCC-IN
The DCC-IN consists of delegations from the local and thematic DCCs, with the participation of SURF, which has been assigned a supporting role in the implementation plan. DCCs learn from each other by sharing experiences, best practices, and solutions to common challenges.
